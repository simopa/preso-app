Frequently Asked Questions

How do I bring music into Preso!?

Tap the + button and pick a video from Photos or an audio or video file from Files. You can also record from your device with the iOS screen broadcast feature: tap the record button, start the broadcast, play what you want to study, then stop from the indicator at the top of the screen. The recording appears in Preso!, where you can trim it.

Does slowing down change the pitch?

No. Speed and pitch are independent: you can slow a passage down to 25% and it stays in tune. If you want to change the key, use the pitch controls: semitones for transposing, cents for fine tuning.

How do I loop a passage precisely?

Set the A and B points while listening, then drag their handles on the waveform. Zoom in for more precision and use the small arrows to nudge each point: the more you zoom, the finer the step. You can add a pause between repetitions, or let the loop speed up on its own with progressive speed.

What does track separation do, and why does it download something?

It splits a clip into drums, bass, vocals and other instruments, so you can mute or solo each one. The first time you use it, the app downloads the model it needs (about 23 MB); after that it works offline. Separation runs on your device and works on clips up to 3 minutes: for a longer piece, export the passage you need as a new clip and separate that. A solo instrument such as a saxophone or a guitar ends up in "other instruments" together with piano and keyboards.

How accurate are the detected notes, tempo and key?

They are estimates meant to help your ear, not to replace it. The keyboard shows the notes with the most energy at the point where you stop, and on full chords it may also light up overtones. Tempo can come out at half or double the real value.

The recording has no sound. Why?

Some apps protect their content and silence the audio while the screen is being broadcast. Preso! cannot record protected content. Use Preso! only with material you have the right to record and study.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your clips stay on your device: no servers, no accounts, no analytics. The only network connection is the one-time download of the separation model. See the Privacy Policy.
